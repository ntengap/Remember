Important to know

Local-only is not the same as encrypted storage. Anyone who can access your unlocked device or browser profile may be able to access this app's data. Avoid storing passwords, card PINs, recovery codes or full identity-document numbers.

Use HTTPS when hosting the app and keep exported backups protected. Clearing browser data may erase your records unless you have an export.
